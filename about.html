About this.

spiteware.ai is a catalog of free apps people built because a paid tool wanted their credit card for something small. An AI agent sweeps the internet every morning for new ones, checks them and scores them. Nobody pays to be here, and nothing is sold. The longer version is in four places.

  • Why
    The manifestoSoftware used to be something you bought. Six theses on how that changed, and an invitation to build the damn thing yourself.
  • How
    The rulesHow apps get found, checked and scored, the two gates every listing has to clear, and what a spite score is. All in public.
  • Stats
    Who visitsThe site's own traffic, straight out of Google Analytics: visitors, what they read, where they came from. Nothing rounded up.
  • Talk
    ContactFeedback, a take-down request, a wrong price or a misquote on a card, or anything else. A person reads it.
