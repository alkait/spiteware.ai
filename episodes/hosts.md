# The hosts

Vera and Gus are invented. Their voices are AI and their stories are scripted, and every episode
says so on its end card and in its description. Their voices and delivery are set in
`hosts.json`; this file is who they are, for whoever writes the next script.

Two kinds of fact live here. **Said on air** is canon: never contradict it. **Background** is
there to draw from: it becomes canon the first time an episode uses it, and whoever writes that
episode moves it up in the same change.

## The pair

Vera designed the logo for Gus's bike shop five years ago. He paid her invoice 43 days late. They
have been friends ever since, and she has never let it go. *(Background.)*

**The argument.** Gus believes people who make software should be paid, and that subscriptions are
an honest way to do it. Vera agrees about being paid. What she can't stand is being ambushed: the
price that appears at the last button, the thing that was free until it had users, the monthly
fee for something that is finished. Neither wins for good. Gus concedes cases and keeps the
principle.

## Vera — brings the grudges

**How she talks.** Quick, exact, dry, warm underneath. Counts things. Does most of the talking:
she presents the apps. Never swears.

**Said on air**
- Has a cat called Kerning, who "has never paid for anything". *(2026-09)*
- Knows about Gus's card statement and told him to go through it. *(2026-09)*

**Background**
- Freelance graphic designer, some video and illustration. Six years at an agency, four on her
  own. Pays for every tool herself; does her own invoicing and taxes.
- A spreadsheet of every business expense with a tab called "leeches" for anything recurring. A
  calendar alarm for every free trial. Missed one once: a year of a stock photo site, "the incident".
- Pays happily for typefaces bought once, her accountant, good paper. Against renting, not paying.
- The money person in every friend group; a phone note called "what Dani owes".
- Climbs indoors twice a week. Three years of Italian. A typeface newsletter with about 400 readers.
- A nine-year-old laptop she refuses to replace.
- Lives with Tom, a teacher with no opinions about software.

**Her people:** Dani (owes her since 2019), Sami (younger brother, "I could build that in a
weekend", ships nothing), her dad (hardware shop, receipts in shoeboxes, never subscribed to
anything), her mum (one streaming password, eleven relatives), Priya (premium tier of everything).

## Gus — defends the bill

**How he talks.** Relaxed, low-key, dry, understated. Short lines. He reacts, asks for the next
one, and concedes with a shrug. The user disliked an upbeat, eager Gus and a first voice that
sounded that way; keep him the calm one.

**Said on air**
- Thinks software should cost money. *(2026-09)*
- Sold software for six years; defends per-seat pricing ("a hundred people should pay more than
  two"). A weekend project "used to be an eighteen-month project". *(2026-09)*
- Went through his card statement with a highlighter and found eleven subscriptions; recognised
  nine. One was four capital letters and seven dollars a month: a meditation app he has opened
  twice. He is "going to try it one more time first". *(2026-09)*
- Read the site's manifesto; takes "seat-based pricing is a hate crime" personally. Searched the
  site for Slack and regretted it. *(2026-09)*

**Background**
- Owns a neighbourhood bike shop, Second Gear, seven staff. Pays per seat for the till, the rota,
  bookings, payroll and team chat: the man who sold per-seat now pays it.
- Married to Marta, a nurse, who uses almost no apps and is usually right. Leo, 14, makes videos
  and hits every paywall a teenager without a card can hit. Nina, 9, has a spelling-app streak
  the whole family maintains. An old beagle, Biscuit.
- Signs up for everything, takes the annual plan, forgets. Tips too much.
- Rides every weekend, tracks all of it, has a drawer of gadgets that still work and can't connect.
- Dictates everything to his phone and says the punctuation out loud.

**His people:** Marta, Leo, Nina, Carla (his sister; he made her wedding video at nineteen, three
minutes of it buffet), Pete (brother-in-law, self-hosts everything on a server called Gerald that
goes down when the dryer runs), his mother (four-minute voice notes), Ravi (head mechanic,
distrusts every new tool).

## Storylines

Each episode moves one or two of these a step. The state after the latest episode is in
`ledger.md`.

| Storyline | Where it can go |
|---|---|
| Gus's count | Eleven subscriptions as of 2026-09. Reported now and then; it goes down, sometimes up. |
| The meditation app | He was going to try it once more. Did he? |
| The shop's bill | Seven seats on everything; the team chat renews in January. Principle against invoice. |
| What Dani owes | A total that changes. One day Dani pays. |
| Sami's weekend | He starts something. Months later it works. Does it belong on the site? |
| Leo's channel | Every paywall he hits is a segment. |
| Vera's laptop | Each month something drops support for it. |
| Kerning | Interrupts. Has opinions. |

## Language

American English, to match the dollar prices: apartment, résumé, dryer, math.
