spite·ware noun

/ˈspaɪt.wɛər/  ·  uncountable, like rage

  1. Software written because an existing product charged money for something that should obviously be free. "He wrote spiteware after the third 'upgrade to Pro' popup."
  2. A free clone of a paid tool, built by someone who got fed up. "Notion asked for $10. He shipped spiteware by Sunday."

Origin: spite + -ware. First recorded shortly after a notes app raised its price. See also: revengeware, "I could build that in a weekend."
