The Spiteware Manifesto

Software used to be something you bought.

Then greed figured out how to make you rent every little piece of it.

  1. 01

    With AI, you don't need to be a software developer to make something useful. A decent idea and a free weekend can go surprisingly far.

  2. 02

    It doesn't have to be perfect. Tiny, imperfect apps can solve surprisingly big annoyances.

  3. 03

    One person can now build an alternative to software that once took an entire company.

  4. 04

    Open source it. Give it away. Save someone else the tokens.

  5. 05

    Don't wait for the next startup to solve your problem. Build the damn thing yourself.

    Every useful alternative makes the next paywall a little less powerful.
