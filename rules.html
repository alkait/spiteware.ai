the rules, in public

Nobody pays to be on this list.

No sponsors, no boosted spots, no favours. Here's exactly how an app gets found, checked and scored — and how yours gets in.

01 Every morning, an agent goes looking for grudges

An agent trawls the last 48 hours of everywhere people announce what they built — Show HN, eight subreddits, brand-new GitHub repos, and once a day a wider pass over Reddit, X, YouTube and the open web. It reads each catch properly: the post, the comments under it, the README, the app's own site. Most of them go back in the water.

02 Two gates. Fail either, you're out

  1. Gate 1
    A grudge, in their own words.Quoted verbatim, linked to where they said it.
  2. Gate 2
    Free, with no strings.No trial, no seat cap, no pricing page hiding anywhere.
